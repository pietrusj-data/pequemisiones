It all started at home 🏠

I'm the dad of two girls. I spend part of every week away from them, and I wanted to keep helping with school even when I'm not there: my eldest is starting Year 3 of primary with the ABN method (the grids, number bonds to 10, the 100 grid…), and my youngest is in her last year of preschool and needs a little extra time with her speech.

Since I couldn't find anything that worked the way her school does, I built it myself: an app where I send them "missions" from wherever I am, with a little message the app reads out loud, exercises that mark themselves without ever frustrating them, and fireworks when they get things right. They're completely hooked. Then other parents started asking for the same thing for their kids — so I'm turning it into a service for more families.

How it works

1

Create your child's profile: their school year, the method their school uses and their favourite theme (princesses, unicorns, space, dinos…).

2

Switch on the modules you want from the catalogue: just numbers, numbers and letters, clocks… you decide what they practise.

3

Every day, their mission: generated automatically from how they're doing (reinforces what's hard, stretches what they've mastered). And whenever you like, send one yourself with your own message — the app reads it to them out loud, with your love in it.

4

You see everything: in your parent area — what they did each day, what was tricky and where they shone, exercise by exercise.

Quick questions

What is ABN?

ABN is an open, number-based mental-arithmetic method used in thousands of Spanish schools: children work with whole quantities on open grids ("rejillas") instead of traditional column sums. If your child brings home grids with jumps and columns — that's ABN.

Does my child need to be able to read?

No. In preschool mode everything is spoken out loud, slowly, and there's a button to repeat the instructions as many times as they like.

What if their school doesn't use ABN?

The numbers, letters, clocks and measures modules work for any school. The ABN grids only switch on if you want them.

Is the app in English or Spanish?

The children's activities speak Spanish, matching what they do at school. An English module (first words, bilingual science) is on our roadmap for bilingual-school families.

What data do you keep about my child?

Almost none: a name or nickname you choose (it stays on your tablet) and their game progress under a pseudonym. No photos, no ads, no trackers, and all data is hosted in the EU.

Does it work on any tablet?

Yes: it's a web app — it runs on any tablet or phone (Android or iPad) with nothing to install from app stores. Add it to the home screen and you're done.
