Trust & safety

A dad built this app for his two daughters. Whatever we wouldn't want for them isn't in here. This is exactly how it works underneath.

What you won't find

  • 🚫 No ads. None, from anyone, ever.
  • 🚫 No trackers. No Google Analytics, no social media pixels, no advertising cookies.
  • 🚫 No open chat. Your child can't message anyone, and strangers can't message them.
  • 🚫 No infinite scroll, no autoplay videos. Sessions end. Nothing is designed to hook them.
  • 🚫 No in-game purchases. Gems are earned by doing exercises and can never be bought with real money.
  • 🚫 No personal data about your child on our servers. More on that below.

What we know about your child (spoiler: almost nothing)

Their name or nickname, school year, favourite theme and gems live only on your device. On our server we store their progress under an opaque family code such as NUBE-K7M2-Q4RT, which doesn't say who you are.

We never ask for surnames, photos, location, contacts or date of birth. Your child has no account: the relationship is with the adult.

💌 The message channel is protected

The loveliest thing about PequeMissions is that you can send a mission with a message and the app reads it aloud to your child. Because that channel goes straight to a small child, we treat it with the care it deserves.

Before your child hears it, every message goes through an automated check looking for insults, threats or content that isn't right for a child. It's a computer check (artificial intelligence), it takes a few seconds, and it works like this:
  • If everything is fine — the normal case — the mission arrives right away.
  • If something doesn't fit, the message is held back: your child sees nothing odd, and the adult gets a notice in their panel to rewrite it.
  • When in doubt, we hold it. We'd rather inconvenience you than take a risk with them.

What's more, there are no private messages between an adult and the child on PequeMissions: everything sent stays visible in the history, with its date, to any adult in the family. In separated households, that matters a great deal.

No automated check is perfect, and we don't promise it catches everything. We're describing a mechanism, not a miracle.

💡 The hint helper

When your child gets stuck they can tap the hint button and the mascot gives them a hand. It's designed with very clear limits:

  • ✅ It hints at the method — the next small step. It never gives the answer.
  • ✅ It isn't a chatbot. Your child doesn't write or speak: they tap a button and get a hint about the exercise in front of them. No conversation, no memory, no detours.
  • ✅ You see everything. Your panel shows every hint they asked for and the reply.
  • ✅ Asking for help costs nothing. They earn their gem all the same: what matters is getting there.

⏳ Their time matters too

You can set a daily time limit (15, 20 or 30 minutes). When it's reached, the mascot says a warm goodbye until tomorrow — and never cuts a round off halfway. Rewards are earned through effort and consistency, never through screen time or beating anyone else. We want your child to play a little, and well.

If something goes wrong

This is maintained by a family, not a multinational. If you spot something that doesn't sit right — a bug, a message that shouldn't have got through, a question about your data — write to hola@pequemisiones.com and you'll get a real reply.
