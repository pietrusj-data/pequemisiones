3 · Children

PequeMissions is designed for children aged 3 to 8, but the account holder is always an adult. Processing the data of a child below the applicable age of digital consent (14 in Spain, 13–16 depending on the country) requires the consent of the holder of parental responsibility. Therefore:

  • Only an adult creates profiles, chooses modules and sends missions.
  • When creating a profile you confirm that you are the child's parent or legal guardian.
  • The child cannot register, message anyone, or provide data on their own.
  • The name shown on screen is chosen by you and can be a nickname. We recommend it is.

5 · How we use artificial intelligence

We're upfront about this because it's the right thing to do, and because European AI rules require it:

  • Message checking: the text an adult writes is sent to Anthropic to be classified as appropriate or not before it reaches the child. Nobody at PequeMissions routinely reads those messages.
  • Hint helper: the hints your child receives are generated automatically, not by a person. They only point at the method for the exercise. There is no free conversation and no memory between hints.
  • No automated decisions with legal effects: no system assesses your child, grades them or decides their educational level. Free-play difficulty is adjusted by a simple rule on the device itself, and missions always use the level the adult chooses.

6 · Cookies

This website uses no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies. No Google Analytics, no social media pixels, no profiling. That's why you won't see an annoying cookie banner.

The app does use your browser's local storage to remember profiles, gems and settings on your own device. This is strictly necessary for the app to work and doesn't require consent. You can clear it by clearing your browsing data (you'll lose local profiles).

7 · Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability at any time, and withdraw your consent where that is the legal basis. Write to hola@pequemisiones.com telling us what you need and, if relevant, your family code. We reply within one month at most.

Since we hold no identifying data about the child, the fastest way to erase all their progress is to ask us to delete a specific family code: it is removed completely.

If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es), C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid, or to the supervisory authority in your own country.

8 · Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and the database enforces row-level access rules: without the family code, nothing belonging to that family can be read or modified. Even so, no system is infallible; if a security breach affected your data we would inform you and notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours, as the GDPR requires.
